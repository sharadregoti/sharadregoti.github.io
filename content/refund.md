**Last updated:** August 7, 2026

This policy applies to paid subscriptions for products offered by Sharad Regoti, including [Get End Of Life](https://getendoflife.com), [Selective Blur](https://selectiveblur.xyz), and [Grammar Coach](https://grammarcoach.in).

## Cancellation

You can cancel your subscription at any time, effective at the end of your current billing period. You will retain access to the product until the end of the period you have already paid for. No partial-month or pro-rated refunds are issued for cancelling mid-cycle.

## Refunds

Charges already billed for the current billing period are **non-refundable**. This applies to both monthly and any other billing intervals offered.

If you believe you were charged in error (e.g. a duplicate charge or a billing system fault), contact me within 30 days of the charge and I will review and correct it.

## Free Trials

Where a product offers a free trial, you will not be charged until the trial ends, and you can cancel any time during the trial to avoid being charged.

## How to Cancel

You can cancel directly from within the product (if a self-service option is available) or by emailing [sharadregoti15@gmail.com](mailto:sharadregoti15@gmail.com) with the product name and the email address used for your subscription.

## Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to [sharadregoti15@gmail.com](mailto:sharadregoti15@gmail.com) or see the [Contact](/contact/) page for full details.
